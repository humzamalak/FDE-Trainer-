# The FDE interview loop
<!-- minutes: 40 -->

Loops differ by company and change often. Check each company's process with the recruiter. A typical loop:

| Stage | What they test | How to prepare here |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiter screen (30 min) | Background, motivation: **why forward-deployed work?** | Write a 60-second "why FDE" answer |
| Technical screen | Coding (LeetCode Easy/Medium or practical) | Track 3 + Mock: Live coding |
| Take-home (some companies) | Build something small for a "customer"; code quality, judgement, communication | Lesson 2 of this track |
| Coding round | Live coding, often practical, talking aloud | Mock: Live coding |
| System design | Customer-focused design, often AI systems | Track 5 + Mock: System design |
| Customer case / decomposition | Discovery, scoping, communication | Track 6 + Mock: Customer case |
| Project deep dive / presentation | Something you built and owned; deep questions | Your H200 / RAG project |
| Hiring manager / behavioural | Ownership, judgement, values | STAR bank + Mock: Behavioural |

## "Why FDE?" — a good answer has

1. What you enjoy: working directly with users on real problems and shipping.
2. Evidence: you already do this (researchers, RONIN, the TRE, the H200 portal).
3. Why this company: something specific about its product or customers.

## Readiness checklist

Do not start applying to your top companies until most of these are true:

- [ ] I solve 3 of 4 random LeetCode Mediums in 35 minutes, talking aloud.
- [ ] I have shipped portfolio project 1 (RAG with evals) and project 2 (H200 inference service) with measured results.
- [ ] I can design an air-gapped LLM deployment with numbers in 45 minutes.
- [ ] I have 8 STAR stories and a calm answer about my CV gaps.
- [ ] I passed each mock interview type at least 3 times with "lean hire" or better.

Apply to "tier B" companies first to practise, then your top choices.

:::written Your "why FDE" answer
Write your 60-second answer to "Why do you want a forward-deployed role, and why here?" Pick a real company you would apply to.
--- rubric ---
- Clear motivation for customer-facing engineering, not just "I like AI"
- Concrete evidence from his own work (researcher support, RONIN, TRE, H200 cluster portal)
- Something specific and accurate about the chosen company's product or customers
- About 120–160 words, easy to say in 60 seconds, confident tone
:::

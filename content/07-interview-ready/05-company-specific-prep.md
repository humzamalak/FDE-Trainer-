# Company-specific preparation
<!-- order: 2.7 -->
<!-- minutes: 40 -->

"Forward Deployed Engineer" means different jobs at different companies. Before each application, spend 2–3 hours finding out **which kind of FDE role it is** and **what their loop tests**, then adjust your practice for the last week.

## The main kinds of FDE employer

| Kind | What the job is | What the loop usually stresses |
|---|---|---|
| **Data/ops platform companies** (the original FDE model, for example Palantir) | Deploy and adapt a large platform inside government and enterprise customers | Decomposition of vague problems, practical coding, learning fast, customer judgement |
| **AI labs and AI platform companies** | Help enterprise customers build production LLM systems on the company's models | Coding, LLM system design, evals, customer communication, sometimes a take-home |
| **AI start-ups selling to enterprises** | Be the engineer who makes each new customer successful; often build product too | Practical coding (APIs, data), a take-home or pairing exercise, ownership, speed |
| **Consultancies and partners** (for example roles built on one platform such as Palantir Foundry or Salesforce) | Deliver projects on a specific platform for clients | The platform, client delivery, sometimes certifications |

These are patterns reported by candidates and job posts, not rules. Each company changes its loop. **Always check with the recruiter**: "Can you tell me the stages and what each one tests?" Recruiters usually answer, and it is not a strange question.

## Research checklist

:::checklist Before each application
- [ ] Read the job post twice; list the 5 skills it asks for and map each to my evidence (a project, a work example)
- [ ] Read the company's engineering blog, docs or customer stories; know 2 real customers and what they use the product for
- [ ] Used the product (free tier, demo, public docs) and wrote 3 observations or questions
- [ ] Found the interview stages (recruiter, the company careers page, candidate reports) and confirmed them with the recruiter
- [ ] Prepared "why this company" in 3 specific sentences (not "AI is exciting")
- [ ] Chose the portfolio project closest to their customers for the deep dive
- [ ] Did one mock of each round type they use, in the last week
- [ ] Prepared 5 questions for interviewers (see below)
:::

## Questions to ask them

- "What does a typical first 3 months look like for an FDE here?"
- "How is the time split between customer sites, remote work and building product?"
- "How much travel, and how much on-call?"
- "How do FDE learnings get back into the product?"
- "What separates an FDE who does well here from one who struggles?"

## Adjust the last week

- Decomposition-heavy loop → two Decomposition mocks and one Customer case.
- LLM-heavy loop → re-do lessons 4.3–4.5 and 4.10, one System design mock on an AI system.
- Take-home → lesson 7.2, and practise a 4-hour timed build with a README.
- Platform consultancy → learn the platform's basics from its public training.

:::written Company brief
Pick a real FDE job post. Write a one-page company brief: what the company sells and to whom, what the FDE does there, the likely interview stages (and how you will confirm them), your evidence for each required skill, your "why this company", and your plan for the last week.
--- rubric ---
- Specific about the product and customers (not generic AI language)
- Maps each required skill to concrete evidence, and names honest gaps with a plan
- Says how the interview stages will be confirmed (recruiter) rather than assuming
- A focused last-week practice plan that matches the loop
:::

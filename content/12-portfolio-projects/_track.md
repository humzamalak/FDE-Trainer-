# 4b. Portfolio projects
<!-- order: 4.5 -->
<!-- skill: ai -->
<!-- weeks: 12-24 -->

Three guided projects that prove you can do the job: an LLM assistant with evals, a data integration service, and an agent with tools and guardrails. Each has milestones with checklists that save your progress. Build them on your own computer, in your own GitHub. Start the first project in week 12 and spend about 3 hours per week on projects until week 24. In interviews, these projects are your deep-dive material.
